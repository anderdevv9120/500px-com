Introduction
Niushoushan Cultural Tourism Zone is located in Jiangning District, Nanjing, Jiangsu Province, where every season brings a new view. Forests, lakes, mountains, and changing light from dawn to dusk create ever-changing scenes throughout the year. Foding Palace, recipient of both the Luban Prize and the Zhan Tianyou Award, offers striking opportunities for architectural photography. Niushoushan is also regarded as the birthplace of the Niutou Zen tradition, while “Spring Outing at Niushou” has been recognized as part of Jiangsu’s intangible cultural heritage. With 83% forest coverage, Niushoushan brings nature, architecture, Zen culture, and local heritage together in one remarkable landscape—waiting to be discovered through the lens. Through Light and Shadow, Niushou Embraces the World invites photography lovers everywhere to step into Niushoushan and discover the countless stories waiting to be captured. Through three creative perspectives — Seasonal Landscapes, Cultural Impressions, and Everyday Discoveries — photographers are invited to document the beauty of the changing seasons, explore the warmth and spirit of local culture, and find unexpected moments in the details of everyday life. Spanning a full year, the competition uses photography as its language to capture the changing face of Niushoushan and create a visual record of the mountain through your own perspective.

Submission Guidelines
Choose one of the following themes for your submission.
01. Lens on Niushoushan
【Key Themes】 On-location photography · International perspectives · Cultural discovery · Experiencing the East 【Overview】 The same mountain can reveal a different story through different cultural perspectives. This theme invites international photographers and visual creators living, working, or traveling in China to experience Niushoushan firsthand and capture the beauty of its landscapes and seasons through their own visual language. Their perspectives offer an authentic and compelling visual record of Niushoushan for audiences around the world. 【Creative Directions】 • Architectural Wonders: The dramatic scale of the Foding Palace dome, the intricate details of Foding Pagoda, and encounters between Eastern and Western architectural aesthetics. • Four Seasons: Capture spring, summer, autumn, and winter at Niushoushan through an individual international perspective, creating a visual series across the seasons. • Cultural Discoveries: Explore Chinese cultural experiences such as Mid-Autumn Festival traditions, blessing rituals, tea culture, and Zen-inspired moments from an international perspective. • Comparative Perspectives: Pair Niushoushan with landscapes from the photographer’s hometown or region to explore the distinctive beauty of different places.
02. Mountains in Focus
【Key Themes】 Niushoushan · Mountains of the world · Forests & valleys · Clouds & mist · People & nature 【Overview】 Niushoushan is a mountain, but it is also a way of seeing mountains. Every mountain has its own height, texture, landscape, and cultural meaning, yet mountains around the world share clouds, changing light, and traces of human life. Starting from Niushoushan, this theme invites photographers around the world to capture mountains, forests, valleys, sea of clouds, and natural landscapes encountered in their hometowns, cities, and travels. Together, these images explore the many faces of mountains around the world and the ways people live with, encounter, and connect with nature. 【Creative Directions】 • Mountains, forests, valleys, sea of clouds, and natural landscapes. • Famous mountains and urban mountain landscapes across different regions and cultures. • Religious architecture, hiking trails, viewpoints, and other traces of human presence in the mountains. • Seasonal mountain landscapes, changing light, clouds, mist, and journeys through the mountains. • Moments of people living with, encountering, or looking out at mountains and landscapes.
03. Towers in Focus
【Key Themes】 Foding Pagoda · Pagodas · Historic towers · Bell towers · Religious architecture · Vertical landmarks 【Overview】 Niushoushan has Foding Pagoda, while cultures around the world have their own towers and vertical landmarks. Towers are points where the earth reaches toward the sky, carrying traces of faith, cities, and civilization. Inspired by Foding Pagoda, this theme invites photographers around the world to search for their own “towers” — pagodas, historic towers, bell towers, church spires, minarets, urban landmarks, and other architectural forms defined by their vertical presence. Let towers from different places and cultures meet through photography. 【Creative Directions】 • Pagodas & Historic Towers: Tower architecture across different cultures, including their forms, proportions, materials, and traces of history. • Religious Architecture: Pagodas, church spires, mosque minarets, and other structures with spiritual or symbolic significance. • Urban Landmarks: TV towers, observation towers, skyscrapers, and other modern structures with a strong vertical presence. • Towers & People: Moments of people encountering, looking up at, walking around, or viewing towers from afar.
04. Moments of Stillness
【Key Themes】 Zen · Restorative spaces · Quiet architecture · Negative space · Light & shadow · Solitude 【Overview】 Niushoushan offers more than beautiful scenery. It is also a place where people can slow down, find stillness, and reconnect with themselves. In an increasingly noisy world, quiet moments have become more meaningful. Inspired by the Zen-like and restorative atmosphere of Niushoushan, this theme invites photographers around the world to capture moments that make us pause: a quiet building, a beam of morning light, a still body of water, a moment of solitude, or an undisturbed connection between people and nature. 【Creative Directions】 • Quiet Spaces: Temples, churches, libraries, museums, and other spaces with a peaceful atmosphere. • Negative Space in Nature: Morning mist, still water, mountain silhouettes, snow-covered landscapes, and minimalist scenes. • Moments of Light: Morning light, sunset, blue hour, and quiet moments shaped by light and shadow. • Solitude: Moments of meditation and reflection, as well as undisturbed connections between people and nature.
Submission Rules
1. Eligible Works All submissions must be photographed within Niushoushan Cultural Tourism Zone and must align with the overall competition theme and the selected submission category. 2. Format Photos may be taken with either a camera or smartphone. Both color and black-and-white works are welcome. Participants may submit single images or photo series. Each series may contain up to 【9 images】 and should focus on the same theme or shooting location. 3. Image Description Each submission must include a brief description of 20–200 Chinese characters or approximately 10–100 English words**, including the shooting date, location, creative approach, and the story behind the work. 4. Originality All submissions must be the participant’s original work. Fully AI-generated images are not accepted. Basic post-processing that does not alter the authenticity of the original image, such as color correction, cropping, and noise reduction, is permitted. Compositing, image stitching, and excessive manipulation are not permitted. 5. Copyright & Permissions Submissions must not infringe upon the copyright, portrait rights, privacy rights, or other legal rights of any third party. For works featuring clearly identifiable individuals, participants must ensure that they have obtained the necessary consent from the people photographed. Selected and winning participants may be required to provide relevant release documentation and high-resolution original files. 6. On-Site Photography & Verification All submissions must be photographed on-site at Niushoushan during the competition period. The shooting date will be verified against the image’s EXIF data. Participants must retain the original RAW or JPEG files for verification. Plagiarism, proxy photography, borrowing or submitting another person’s work, or any other form of misrepresentation is strictly prohibited and will result in disqualification. Participants must provide the shooting date and camera position/location when submitting their work. For organized photography sessions, as well as participants who visit independently, entry records may be cross-checked against the scenic area’s ticketing and access-control records for verification. Participants are encouraged to submit photos taken during the competition period.

Judging Criteria and Process
The competition will be evaluated in two stages: Final Award Selectionand Quarterly Showcases. 【Final Award Selection】 At the end of the submission period, the platform’s editorial team will conduct an initial review of all entries to identify works that meet the submission requirements and align with the competition themes. Selected works will advance to the final judging stage, where the jury will evaluate each shortlisted entry based on the criteria below and determine the final winners. 【Quarterly Showcases】 Throughout the competition, 20–30 outstanding works will be selected each quarter for public showcase. These quarterly showcases are intended to provide additional exposure for selected works and do not affect or determine the final award results. 【Judging Criteria】 • Theme Relevance — 30% How clearly the work captures Niushoushan and connects with the selected competition theme. • Storytelling & Emotional Impact — 25% How authentically and meaningfully the work conveys a story, atmosphere, or emotional connection. • Visual Expression — 25% The strength of composition, light, color, and overall visual presentation. • Originality & Distinctiveness — 20% The uniqueness of the photographer’s perspective, observation, and personal visual expression.
