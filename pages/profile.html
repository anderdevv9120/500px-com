Leading workshops from pole to pole. Skylum Global Ambassador Marco Grassi is an award-winning photographer focused on landscape photography as well as an avid adventurer and expedition leader. He's traveled the world extensively to capture the beauty around him, driven by his interest in pristine landscapes and remote destinations which has led him to some of the most incredible places on Earth. As a photo educator leading workshops all around the world, he arranges adventures for those seeking spectacular landscapes and intense experiences like him. Marco’s renowned work has been published and rewarded extensively worldwide in a large variety of media outlets.
